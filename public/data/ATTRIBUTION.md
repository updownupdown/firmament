# Data sources

Firmament's data files are generated by `scripts/build_data.py` from these sources.

| File | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `stars-bright.bin`, `stars-faint.bin`, `stars-meta.json` | [HYG database v4.1](https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg) by David Nash (astronexus), compiled from Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star and Gliese catalogs | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |
| `cultures/*.json` | [Stellarium sky cultures](https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium-skycultures) (only the cultures licensed CC BY-SA; authors are listed in each file) | CC BY-SA |
| `tycho/*.bin`, and the fine structure of `milkyway.png` | [Tycho-2 catalogue](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/hipparcos/tycho-2) (Høg et al. 2000, ESA), via [python-tycho2](https://github.com/lkangas/python-tycho2) by Lauri Kangas | Tycho-2: free to use with acknowledgement; python-tycho2: MIT |
| `boundaries.json`, `dsos.json`, `milkyway.png` | [d3-celestial](https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial) by Olaf Frohn | BSD 3-Clause |
| `vsop87b.json` | VSOP87 (Bretagnon & Francou 1988), via [astronomia](https://github.com/commenthol/astronomia) | MIT (astronomia) |
| `elp-mpp02.json` | ELP/MPP02 (Chapront & Francou 2003), via astronomia | MIT (astronomia) |
| `deltat.json` | ΔT from USNO / IERS, via astronomia | MIT (astronomia) |
| Orbital elements of asteroids and trans-Neptunian objects (in `src/astro/kepler.ts`) | [Minor Planet Center](https://minorplanetcenter.net), as collected in Stellarium's [`ssystem_minor.ini`](https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/blob/master/data/ssystem_minor.ini) | Facts; MPC data is free to use with acknowledgement |
| `textures/*.jpg`, `textures/saturn-rings.png` (surface maps of the Sun, planets and Moon; Saturn's rings) | [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), based on NASA mission data | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `dso-photos/*.jpg` (photographs of 108 galaxies, nebulae and clusters, and where they sit on the sky) | ESO, ESA/Hubble, NASA and the Sloan Digital Sky Survey, as selected and placed by [Stellarium](https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/master/nebulae/default); each image's own credit and source page is in `dso-photos/index.json` | ESO and ESA/Hubble: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); NASA: public domain; SDSS: free to use with credit to the Sloan Digital Sky Survey |
| `art/*.jpg` (constellation illustrations) | [Stellarium](https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/master/skycultures/modern) modern sky culture artwork, by Johan Meuris | [Free Art License 1.3](https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/) |
| `landscapes/moon.webp` | Apollo 17 panorama of the Taurus–Littrow valley, NASA, as prepared for Stellarium | Public domain (NASA) |
| `landscapes/mars.webp` | "Husband Hill Summit" panorama by the rover Spirit, NASA/JPL-Caltech/Cornell, as prepared for Stellarium by Michael Smith | Public domain (NASA/JPL) |
| Mean orbital elements of the moons of Mars, Saturn and Uranus (in `src/astro/satellites.ts`) | Fitted to the BDL and TASS theories as implemented in [PyEphem](https://rhodesmill.org/pyephem/) 4.2 (2000–2038); Triton from Jacobson (2009) and Charon from Brozović et al. (2015), via Stellarium's `ssystem_major.ini` | Facts |
| `fonts/outfit-*.woff2` (the app's typeface) | [Outfit](https://github.com/Outfitio/Outfit-Fonts) by the Outfit Project Authors, via Fontsource | [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org) |

Modified: catalogs were filtered, truncated and re-encoded. Barnard's Star's
proper motion in declination is restored to its Hipparcos value (HYG caps it at
9999.99 mas/yr). Tycho-2 stars already in HYG were removed. The planet maps were resized
and re-encoded, and Saturn's ring texture cut to one radial row. The deep-sky photos and constellation artwork were re-encoded as JPEG (the artwork in greyscale) and the landscapes as WebP. The Milky Way map
blends d3-celestial's brightness contours with Tycho-2 star counts. The data files in this folder are shared under the same
licenses as their sources.
